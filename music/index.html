Our AI labeling policy

We use AI tools as part of how we make music, and we are open about it. This is how we handle it on every release.

  • A person is behind every song. Anatoliy Goloborodko writes the lyrics, directs the sound, and picks every track we release. The concept and story behind each artist are human-made.
  • We label AI use on every release. Each release is marked as fully AI-generated, AI-assisted, or no AI, in the way each store asks for.
  • Our personas are our own characters. AI-assisted artists such as One Kallio, Ruby Saltrana, Ladder Erah and Meesha Vox are fictional characters created by ADKO Games. We say so in their profiles.
  • We never imitate real artists. We do not copy or clone the voice, name or style of any real performer, and we do not use other people's recordings.
  • We own what we release. Anatoliy LLC owns 100% of the masters and publishing for every song in our catalog.
  • We follow each platform's rules. When a store updates its AI or content rules, we update our releases to match.

Questions or concerns about a release? Email anatoliy@adkogames.com.
