Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

ADKO Games is a game studio owned and operated by Anatoliy LLC. This policy explains what information Anatoliy LLC ("we", "us") collects through adkogames.com and our games, including Super Style Volley, and how we use it.

This website

adkogames.com does not ask you for personal information and does not sell data. Like any website, our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested) for security and to keep the site running. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when the page loads.

Visit counter. To see how many people visit, we use Cloudflare Web Analytics. It does not use cookies, does not identify you and does not follow you to other sites. It only gives us totals, such as visits per day, which pages were viewed, the country and the type of device or browser. Cloudflare's own privacy policy explains how it handles this data.

Spam protection. Our email address is hidden from bots. When you click "Show email address" or an email button, Cloudflare Turnstile runs a quick check that you are a person. Turnstile does not use cookies to track you and is not used for ads; Cloudflare's privacy policy explains how it handles this check.

Google Ads. We advertise our games with Google Ads. This site uses the Google Ads tag, which sets cookies, to measure how many people reach our pages after seeing or clicking one of our ads. Google may use these cookies to show ads and to count ad results; it does not give us your name or contact details. You can turn off personalised ads at adssettings.google.com, and Google's privacy policy explains how it uses this data.

Playtest sign-up

If you join a playtest, you fill in a Google Form. We use what you send us (for example your name, email address and the device you play on) only to contact you about the playtest and to improve the game. We don't sell it or share it with anyone else, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

Email list

If you join our email list, we keep three things: your email address, your consent to receive our emails (and when you gave it), and your age group. We use the age group only to make sure our emails are right for you. We use your email address only to send you news about our games, playtests and updates. We don't sell it or share it with anyone else.

Leaving the list. You can leave at any time by using the unsubscribe link in any of our emails, or by emailing us at Show email address. When you leave, we stop emailing you and delete your details from the list.

Super Style Volley

No account needed. You don't sign up or give us your name, email address or phone number to play.

Your progress stays on your device. Your levels, cards, coins, gems, outfits, settings and purchase records are saved on your own phone. We don't receive a copy. If you delete the game, that progress is deleted with it.

Your age. The first time you open the game, it asks for your birth year, once. The answer stays on your device and is used to switch on the right protections before any ads or analytics start. We only receive an age group (under 13, 13 to 15, or 16 and over), never your birth year.

Online play. When you play online or with a friend, the game connects through Photon, a multiplayer service run by Exit Games. To join players together, Photon handles your in-game nickname, a random player ID, the room or friend code you use, the hero and ball you pick, the moves of the match, and basic technical information such as your IP address, region and device type. This is used only to run matches. There is no text chat, only emotes. Your nickname is shown to the players you play against, so please don't use your real name or anything personal as a nickname. Players under 13 can't type a nickname; the game gives them a made-up one. Photon's own privacy policy explains how it treats that information.

Ads. The free game shows ads through Google AdMob. You can remove them with a No Ads purchase. Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device information and how you interact with ads, to show and measure ads. How ads work depends on your age:

  • Under 13: only ads meant for children, rated G at most, and never personalized.
  • 13 to 15: ads are never personalized.
  • 16 and over: where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), the game asks your permission with Google's consent message before showing personalized ads. On iPhone, Apple's "Allow tracking" request may appear together with that message, and only for players 16 and over; it is never shown to younger players.

Where the law requires it, the game's settings include an AD PRIVACY button that reopens Google's privacy options so you can change your choice at any time. You can also reset or limit your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Google's privacy policy explains how it uses this data.

Analytics. We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is played, for example which screens are opened, how matches go and which features are used. Analytics only starts after the age question. It collects an app instance ID, device model, operating system, country, game events (such as matches finished, levels reached, heroes picked, shop visits, purchases and ads watched) and your age group. It never collects your advertising ID. For players under 16, Firebase is set so that this data is never used for advertising or personalization; for players 16 and over, only if you agree. We also use Firebase Remote Config to switch shop items, themes and events on or off for everyone; it does not target you personally. The game has no crash-reporting tool.

Purchases. You can buy gem packs and No Ads packs inside the game, and restore earlier purchases. Purchases are made through the Apple App Store or Google Play, which handle the payment. We never see or store your card or bank details. The store tells the game only that a purchase went through, so the item can be added; the transaction ID is kept on your device so purchases can be restored. Receipts and refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their own policies. The game uses Unity's purchasing service to talk to the store. The game also checks which country your store account or SIM card is from, on your device only, to follow local rules (for example, paid random packs are hidden in Belgium).

Promo codes. When you enter a giveaway code, the game sends only that code, the game version and a random one-time ticket to our own server (a Google Apps Script) to check that it is valid and to count how many times it has been used. No device ID or player details are sent.

Feedback and email list. The game links to two optional Google Forms: a feedback form (13 and over) and our email list (16 and over). Anything you type there is covered by the Email list section above and Google's privacy policy. Players under 13 don't see the feedback form or the rating pop-up.

The free demo. The browser demo, also offered on itch.io, has no purchases and saves progress in your browser. When you play on itch.io, itch.io's own privacy policy applies to what itch.io collects.

Changes to the game. If a later version adds accounts, cloud saves or other new data use, we will update this policy before that version is released and explain exactly what is collected and why. Where the law or the app store requires it, the game will ask for your permission first.

Children

Super Style Volley is made for a general audience, and it has extra protections for younger players. For players under 13, the game shows only child-directed, non-personalized ads, gives them a made-up online name, hides the feedback form and rating pop-up, and never asks for consent or tracking. We do not knowingly collect personal information such as names or email addresses from children under 13. Purchases go through the app store, where parents can use the store's own parental controls to approve or block them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Social media

When you follow or message us on TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, X or other platforms, those platforms' own privacy policies apply.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by contacting us below. Because game progress is kept only on your device, deleting the game deletes it. You can change your ad choices with the AD PRIVACY button in the game (where available) or in your phone's settings.
