Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

ADKO Games is a game studio owned and operated by Anatoliy LLC. This policy explains what information Anatoliy LLC ("we", "us") collects through adkogames.com and our games, including Super Style Volley, and how we use it.

This website

adkogames.com does not ask you for personal information, does not use advertising or tracking cookies, and does not sell data. Like any website, our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested) for security and to keep the site running. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when the page loads.

Playtest sign-up

If you join a playtest, you fill in a Google Form. We use what you send us (for example your name, email address and the device you play on) only to contact you about the playtest and to improve the game. We don't sell it or share it with anyone else, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

Super Style Volley

No account needed. You don't sign up or give us your name, email address or phone number to play.

Your progress stays on your device. Your levels, cards, coins, gems, outfits and settings are saved on your own phone or computer. We don't receive a copy. If you delete the game, that progress is deleted with it.

Online play. When you play online or with a friend, the game connects through Photon, a multiplayer service run by Exit Games. To join players together, Photon handles your in-game nickname, a random player ID, the room or friend code you use, the moves of the match, and basic technical information such as your IP address, region and device type. This is used only to run matches. Your nickname is shown to the players you play against, so please don't use your real name or anything personal as a nickname. Photon's own privacy policy explains how it treats that information.

Purchases. You can buy gems inside the game. Purchases are made through the Apple App Store (or Google Play on Android), which handles the payment. We never see or store your card or bank details. The store tells the game only that a purchase went through, so the gems can be added. Purchase receipts and refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their own policies. The game uses Unity's purchasing service to talk to the store. The game also checks which country your App Store account is from, on your device only, to follow local rules (for example, which options are shown in Belgium).

No ads, no tracking. The current version shows no advertising, has no analytics or crash-reporting tools turned on, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

The free demo. The browser demo, also offered on itch.io, has no purchases and saves progress in your browser. When you play on itch.io, itch.io's own privacy policy applies to what itch.io collects.

Changes to the game. If a later version adds accounts, cloud saves, advertising or analytics, we will update this policy before that version is released and explain exactly what is collected and why. Where the law or the app store requires it, the game will ask for your permission first.

Children

Super Style Volley is made for a general audience, not specifically for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Purchases go through the app store, where parents can use the store's own parental controls to approve or block them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Social media

When you follow or message us on TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, X or other platforms, those platforms' own privacy policies apply.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by contacting us below. Because game progress is kept only on your device, deleting the game deletes it.
