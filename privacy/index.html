Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

ADKO Games is a game studio owned and operated by Anatoliy LLC. This policy explains what information Anatoliy LLC ("we", "us") collects through adkogames.com and our games, including Super Style Volley, and how we use it.

This website

adkogames.com does not ask you for personal information, does not use advertising or tracking cookies, and does not sell data. Like any website, our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested) for security and to keep the site running. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when the page loads.

Playtest sign-up

If you join a playtest, you fill in a Google Form. We use what you send us (for example your name, email address and the device you play on) only to contact you about the playtest and to improve the game. We don't sell it or share it with anyone else, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

Our games

The current Super Style Volley demo does not require an account and does not collect personal information. Your progress is saved on your own device. Our free demo is also offered on itch.io; when you play there, itch.io's own privacy policy applies to what itch.io collects.

If a future version of a game adds accounts, cloud saves, purchases or analytics, we will update this policy before that version is released and explain exactly what is collected and why.

Children

Our games are made for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Social media

When you follow or message us on TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, X or other platforms, those platforms' own privacy policies apply.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by contacting us below.
